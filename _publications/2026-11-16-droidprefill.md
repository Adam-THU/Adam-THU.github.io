**Zhe Dong**, Guanyu Cai, Yuchen Jiang, and Jiliang Wang

DroidPrefill is an architecture for running mobile GUI agents entirely on smartphone NPUs. It combines prefill-centric inference with efficient context management to reduce autoregressive decoding and keep decision-critical information within a fixed compiled context budget.

In controlled comparisons with the autoregressive T3A+ baseline on commercial smartphones, DroidPrefill reduces per-step inference latency by up to **85.0%** while improving task success rate by up to **24.6%**.

This work is a **preprint**.
